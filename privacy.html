Privacy.

How we handle information when you visit our website, get in touch or book a session.

When you contact us

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address and message so we can respond. You can also provide a phone number, and your selected training plan is included when you arrive from the pricing page. FormSubmit processes the information you submit and delivers it to our inbox at buffysgym@gmail.com. You can also email us directly.

Please don’t include health information, payment details or other sensitive information in the form. We can discuss the best way to share anything needed for your training when we reply.

FormSubmit’s documentation says it keeps an archive of submissions for 30 days. Read its privacy policy and submission archive information. That archive is separate from messages delivered to our email inbox.

Bookings and payments

Our booking and purchase links take you to Momence. Information you enter there is handled through Momence’s booking and payment services. We use booking information to manage your classes and training sessions. Read Momence’s privacy policy for details about its services.

Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub processes technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to provide and protect its services. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

We use your browser’s local storage to remember your optional-cookie choice. Declining optional Meta cookies does not affect the enquiry form or our Momence booking links.

Optional Meta cookies

If you choose Allow, this site loads Meta Pixel to help us understand visits and measure ads. Meta can receive page-view information, including the page address, referring page, IP address and browser information. It may use cookies to associate visits with a browser or an ad click, and with information it holds through its own services.

We configure the Pixel to send PageView events. We do not add the contents of your enquiry to those events. Meta’s privacy policy explains how it handles information.

The optional cookies include _fbp, a browser identifier, and _fbc, an ad-click identifier when present. Meta Pixel stays off unless you allow it.

Your choices and questions

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings. Choosing Decline stops this site’s optional Pixel tracking and removes its _fbp and _fbc cookies where this site can access them. It does not recall information already sent to Meta.

For questions about information you have shared with Buffy’s Gym, or to request access, correction or deletion, email buffysgym@gmail.com.
